## R3. Stop-on-error and repeat-run estimates

| Script | `set -e` or equivalent? | Repeat-run estimate (not tested) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `live_selftest.sh` | **No.** `set -u` catches unset parameters, not failed commands (32,45–51). It checks key acquisition statuses, accumulates failures, and returns based on the failure count (59–70,75–89,100–147,150–159,169–215). Output-file failures do not feed the tally (203–215). | Synthetic fixture setup/cleanup runs afresh. | `mac_install.sh` | **No.** `set -u` plus selective `die` guards (12,37–44,55–56,75–77,92–111,118); some moves, cleanup and successful completion messaging are not guarded, and failed `bootstrap` is a note (104,112–114,126–129,138–145). | Plan reprints; dry-run rechecks release metadata. ## R4. Ranked gaps

1. **The installer can leave a partly changed machine.** The version-only skip does not verify app bytes, some rename/cleanup results are unchecked, and an already loaded agent is not reloaded after a plist rewrite. “Done” can follow a load failure (`scripts/hass/mac_install.sh:84–115,118–147`; compare the repeat-run wording at `docs/hass/MAC_MINI_FINAL_STEPS.md:38–42`).
2. **Self-test status does not prove its artifacts were saved.** Growth write errors are ignored, log and brief failures do not change the final failure count, and the destination options are not restricted to a scratch or output directory (`scripts/hass/live_selftest.sh:47–50,194–215`).
3. **The Mac checker is evidence about probes, not proof of full setup.** Its Messages result is a hint, an installed app can pass with unknown version, and native Mac behavior is explicitly unverified in the source notes (`scripts/hass/mac_setup_check.sh:5–6,32–50`; `docs/hass/MAC_MINI_FINAL_STEPS.md:73–77`).
